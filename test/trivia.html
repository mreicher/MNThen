        How it works

        1. Play three rounds

          Each round has 5 questions, for 15 in all. There is a short ad break after rounds one and two.

        2. Beat the clock

          A question starts at 100 points and drops over 30 seconds. You earn what is left when you answer correctly.

        3. Watch answers fall

          Wrong answers drop off the board as time passes. Two answers always remain, so the last choice is yours.

        4. Choose once

          Your first choice is final. A wrong answer or running out of time scores zero, with no penalty.

        Accounts and scores

        • Guests can play any time. Guest scores are not saved.
        • Account holders must verify their email before playing signed in.
        • A score counts only when you finish the whole game.
        • The leaderboard ranks your best game and your all-time points. Ties share a place.
        • Offensive names and unfair scores may be removed.
