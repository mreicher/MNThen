Game Rules & Guidelines

How to Play

  1. 01Play three rounds of five Minnesota history questions—15 questions total.
  2. 02You have 30 seconds per question. One incorrect option falls away about every 8 seconds; the correct answer stays until time runs out. Answer sooner to earn more points, up to 100.
  3. 03Two short sponsor breaks appear between rounds. Your score carries through the game.
  4. 04Play as a guest for fun, or verify your email to save scores and join the leaderboard.

Account & Leaderboard Rules

  • Be respectful: Your first name, last name, and display name must not contain profanity, slurs, hate speech, or inappropriate language. Accounts with offensive names will be removed.
  • One account per person: Create only one account. Multiple accounts to game the leaderboard are not allowed.
  • Real names preferred: Use your actual first and last name. Impersonation or misleading names may result in account removal.
  • Display name format: The leaderboard shows "First Name Last Initial" with an optional custom name in parentheses.

Email Verification

After creating an account, we send a verification email to the address you provided. You must verify your email before your scores can be saved to the leaderboard.

  • Check your inbox: Look for an email from Minnesota Then with a verification link.
  • Check spam/junk folders: Sometimes verification emails get filtered incorrectly.
  • Resend if needed: If you don't see the email within 10 minutes, use the "Resend email" button on the verification screen.
  • Click the link: Open the verification link in your email to activate your account.

Fair Play

  • No cheating, exploiting bugs, or using automated tools to inflate scores.
  • Leaderboard scores are final and may be removed if foul play is detected.
  • Have fun and learn some Minnesota history!
