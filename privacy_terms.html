Privacy Policy

Your walk through history is yours alone.

Minnesota Then keeps data collection to what is needed to run the project. This policy covers the interactive map, this website, the history trivia game, and our publishing and sponsorship work.

The Short Version

  • The map and online stories are free and need no account. Your location stays on your device.
  • A trivia account is optional and only for people 13 and older. If you create one, part of your name and your scores appear on a public leaderboard.
  • We show sponsor credits and sponsor messages. We do not use ad networks, and we do not sell your personal information.
  • Many pages use Google Analytics to measure traffic.

The Map and Stories Need No Account

You can use the map and read or listen to our online stories without an account, password, email address, or registration.

Your Location and Sensor Data Stay on Your Device

The map uses your browser's Geolocation API to place you on the map and trigger exhibits when you are near a historical location. It may also access your device compass (for heading) and accelerometer (to detect when you begin walking so the map does not drift while you are standing still).

This processing happens inside your browser on your device. We do not receive your GPS coordinates, compass readings, accelerometer data, or movement history.

Anonymous Counts

The map and articles may add to running totals, such as how often a place is visited or how many views or likes an item has. These totals are not stored with your name or location history.

Trivia Game Accounts

You can play the trivia game as a guest. Guest scores are not saved. An account is needed only to save scores and appear on the leaderboard.

Age check

Before showing the signup form, we ask for your birth month and year to check your age. The answer is checked on your device and is not saved or sent to us. If it shows you are under 13, we do not ask for your name or email address, and your browser stores a marker that keeps signup closed for 30 days. You can still play as a guest.

What we collect

  • Your first and last name, and a leaderboard display name if you choose one
  • Your email address and password, handled by Google's Firebase Authentication service. We can see your email address, but not your password.
  • Your high score, all-time points, number of games played, when your account was created, and when you last played
  • Whether you checked the newsletter box

We use this information to sign you in, verify your email address, send password reset messages, save your scores, run the leaderboard, and enforce the game rules. Google processes technical details such as your IP address to provide and protect the sign-in and database services, as described in Firebase's privacy information.

What the public can see

Anyone, signed in or not, can see the leaderboard: your first name, last initial, optional display name, scores, and number of games played. Your email address and full last name are not shown on it.

Newsletter

The newsletter box is optional and unchecked by default. If you check it, we may add your email address to our Substack newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: open your account menu in the game, choose Delete My Account, and confirm with your password. This removes your sign-in, game profile, scores, and leaderboard entry. If you joined the newsletter, unsubscribe through any issue or email us. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

Sharing Your Score

Nothing is posted unless you choose to share. When you pick a social network, email, or another app, your game link (and, where supported, your score) is passed to that service, and its privacy policy applies. Our share options are ordinary links and buttons, not social network tracking code.

Sponsors and Advertising

Sponsorships help support Minnesota Then. You may see sponsor credits on map locations, articles, and our sponsors page, and sponsor messages in breaks during the trivia game. Trivia sponsors rotate and are not chosen based on who you are or what you do online. We do not place ad-network code or advertising beacons on our pages.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not give sponsors information about individual visitors or players. If you follow a sponsor's link, that sponsor's website and privacy policy apply.

What We Store in Your Browser

Our pages keep a small amount of data in your browser, using tools such as localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, and the browser's database. It may include:

  • Your last audio playback position, so you can resume listening
  • Location visit cooldown timestamps, to manage the 2-minute revisit window
  • Install preferences, so we do not repeatedly prompt you to install the app
  • Which trivia questions you have recently seen, to reduce repeats
  • Your trivia sign-in, so you stay signed in until you sign out
  • A marker that trivia signup is closed on this browser after an under-13 answer to the age check. It does not contain your birth date.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser's settings.

Website Analytics

Many pages of this website use Google Analytics to measure aggregate traffic, such as which pages are read and from what general region. Google Analytics may set cookies and receives your IP address and browser details, as described in Google's privacy policy. We use it to understand what readers find useful, not to identify individuals. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the analytics script is not loaded. You can also block it with your browser's settings or a content blocker.

Contact Form and Sponsorship Inquiries

If you write to us through the contact form, we receive the name, email address, subject, and message you enter. Submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service, whose own privacy policy applies to that transmission.

We use what you send to respond to you. If you become a sponsor, we also keep your contact details, materials, agreement, and payment records as needed to carry out the sponsorship and meet bookkeeping and legal obligations.

Newsletter, Support, and Books

Our newsletter is hosted by Substack. As the publisher, we can see the email addresses of subscribers, and Substack's privacy policy applies to your subscription. Voluntary contributions are processed by Buy Me a Coffee, which handles payment details; we see only what it reports to us. Books bought through Amazon or another retailer are handled under that retailer's privacy policy.

Outside Services

Our pages load files such as audio, photographs, map tiles, fonts, and software libraries from outside providers, including Firebase and Google Cloud, OpenStreetMap, Google Fonts, and the cdnjs network. When your browser requests these files, the provider may log your IP address and browser type. We do not control those logs.

Offline Caching

A Service Worker may store app files, map tiles, and audio on your device so the map works offline or in areas with poor signal. You can remove this cache by clearing site data or uninstalling the app.

How Long We Keep Information, and Your Choices

We keep trivia account information until you delete your account or we remove it under the Terms below. We keep messages and sponsorship records only as long as we reasonably need them. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

Security

We limit access to account records and rely on established providers for sign-in and storage. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children's Privacy

Trivia accounts are for people age 13 and older, and signup includes the age check described above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Please do not send us personal information through the contact form or newsletter if you are under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has created an account or sent us information, email mattreicher@protonmail.com. You may ask what we have and have it deleted. When we learn that we have collected a child's information without the required parental consent, we delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and revise the date below. The current policy is always available here.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mattreicher@protonmail.com.
